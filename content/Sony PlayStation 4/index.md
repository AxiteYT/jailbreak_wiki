# Pre-requisites
## 1. Stop existing firmware updates (if they exist)
If a firmware update is currently downloading, we will need to delete it before it has the chance to install.

## 2. Disable automatic updates
To disable automatic updates on your PlayStation 4, please do the following:
1. Open **Settings**
2. Scroll down and open **System**
3. Open **Automatic Downloads**
4. Uncheck every option as seen below
   ![[PS4AutomaticDownloads.png]]
# How to check your current PlayStation 4 Firmware
To check the current firmware installed on your PlayStation 4, please do the following:
1. Open **Settings**
2. Scroll down and open **System**
3. Open **System Information**
You will see your current firmware version next to **System Software** as pictured below
![[PS4FirmwareVersion.png]]
